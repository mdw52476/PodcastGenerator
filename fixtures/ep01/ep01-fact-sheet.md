# EP01 fact sheet: Glen Napoleon Burbage ("Atlantic City John Doe")

Status key: **confirmed** = 2+ independent outlets or a primary source; **reported** = 1 outlet only; **disputed** = sources disagree.
Note on independence: all coverage traces to two releases, from New Jersey State Police (NJSP) and Ramapo College's Investigative Genetic Genealogy (IGG) Center, dated Oct 1, 2026. Outlets add little original reporting.

## Reported only (Hoodline, single source): kept OUT of the script
Retired 1960; final posting Santa Rosa, CA; planned to settle in Charleston with wife and three children; retrieved his luggage before vanishing; body recovered by U.S. Coast Guard after a fishing crew spotted it; Timex watch, Dingo boots, Levi's; surgical pins in arms. Othram Inc. contributed (TomsRiver.org, CountryHerald; minor, omitted).

## Derived
- Age: 59 in March 1981; his 60th birthday (Apr 12) fell 20 days after the March 23 date.
- No suspects, no allegation of foul play, no cause of death. The script must speculate on none of these.

## Dramatization boundary
- **May invent:** the narrator (fictional retired Iowa reporter), his desk, shoebox, his memory of once visiting the Raleigh Ave beach, weather, sea and boardwalk atmosphere, the texture of a classroom.
- **Must stay factual:** every name, date, place, agency, the order of events, the DNA process, the open status of the investigation. No quotes put in any real person's mouth. No theory about how he died.
